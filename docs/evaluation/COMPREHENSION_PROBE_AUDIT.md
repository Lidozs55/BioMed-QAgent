# Comprehension Probe Audit

## Purpose

This document records the comprehension-probe results for the paper text. It is an internal audit of whether a reader can determine the meaning of the text from the specified reading boundary alone. It is not a fact check, style review, or rewrite plan.

A reported item means that the probe could not determine a unique meaning, relationship, or required premise from its allowed text. It does not by itself mean that the underlying implementation or experiment is wrong.

## Probe Protocol

Five chapter groups were checked with isolated subagents:

- Front matter and Chapters 1--2: the complete text was supplied directly to the probe; no file, shell, search, browser, or external-context access.
- Chapter 3: only `ch03-system-overview.tex` was allowed.
- Chapter 4: only `ch04-dataset-core.tex` was allowed.
- Chapter 5: the first file-reading probe failed with `stream_read_error`; it was discarded. A second probe received the complete Chapter 5 text directly and is the authoritative Chapter 5 result.
- Chapter 6: only `ch06-conclusion.tex` was allowed.

The probes were instructed to report only genuine comprehension breaks in the format `位置 / 问题 / 缺失信息`. They were not allowed to use other chapters, project files, outside sources, prior conversation, or domain knowledge to fill gaps. No paper files were modified by the probes.
