## Overall risk

**Moderate.** The core single-request pause/resume and accepted-vs-timeout arbitration are well defended and have strong focused tests. The material residual risk is cross-feature execution: HIL is not an exclusivity boundary, and the newly co-located V2 build tool is neither cancellation-aware nor event-loop friendly. That combination can produce or publish data while the Run is visibly paused for a correction, and can make cancellation appear hung on large builds.
